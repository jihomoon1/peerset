### 2. Methodology 0.4.0: continuing-operations EPS for P/E

**Problem, found in the reconciliation.** JCI's FY2025 net income attributable (3,291) includes a large gain from discontinued operations. Its income from continuing operations attributable to JCI was only **1,721**. LTM diluted EPS built from total EPS (5.03 + 3.08 − 2.42 = 5.69) is therefore inflated by a one-time gain, which makes JCI's P/E misleadingly low. Bankers compute P/E on continuing-operations EPS [convention]. The cash-flow statement's D&A also includes discontinued operations in prior periods. That's why JCI's 9M 2025 D&A (585) exceeds 9M 2026 (495).

**Proposed 0.4.0 text** (put the exact wording in the plan):

- **§5 EPS.**
    - When any LTM component period reports discontinued operations — a nonzero `IncomeLossFromDiscontinuedOperationsNetOfTax…` or a disc-ops per-share fact for that period — LTM diluted EPS uses `IncomeLossFromContinuingOperationsPerDilutedShare`, with the same FY + YTD − prior-YTD bridge. If any component is missing, P/E is **Unsupported** ("continuing-operations EPS not reported"). Add the warning `W_EPS_CONTINUING_OPS` (information), naming the disc-ops amount.
    - Without discontinued operations, nothing changes.
    - The EPS cross-check then uses LTM income from continuing operations attributable to the parent.
- **§3 net income.** Unchanged: it stays net income attributable to the parent, shown as reported. The drawer notes the discontinued-operations amount when there is one.
- **§4 D&A.** When a component period reports discontinued operations, add the caution `W_DA_INCLUDES_DISC_OPS`: "cash-flow D&A may include discontinued operations". The value is not changed.
- Bump to 0.4.0, add a change-log row, update `policy.ts`, and add tests: a synthetic disc-ops case, JCI, and a no-disc-ops company such as LII where nothing changes.

**JCI golden** changes under the drift protocol (blueprint §16): reason "definition change, methodology 0.4.0".
